This is a one-time setup. Stay on your existing Free plan. Your project address and public key are already filled in; no password is included.

1 · PREPARE PRIVATE STORAGE ONCE

Use your own sign-in email

Enter the email you will use inside Governor. It stays in your browser on this page.

Open my storage setup ↗
  1. Press Copy my setup.
  2. Press Open my storage setup. Paste into the empty editor, then press Run.
  3. Wait for the success message. These instructions create a separate private table and leave earlier Governor records in place.

Show setup text if copying is unavailable
Enter your email and press Copy my setup.

2 · CREATE YOUR PERSONAL SIGN-IN ONCE

Your cloud-project login and app login can be different.

  1. Open your sign-in list ↗. If your email is already listed, keep that account.
  2. Otherwise choose Add user → Create new user. Use the email entered above and a password you keep yourself. Enable Auto Confirm User if offered.
  3. In sign-in settings ↗, turn off Allow new users to sign up after creating your own account. Leave email sign-in enabled.

Your app password is used only when you sign in to your project. Do not send it in chat.

3 · CONNECT EACH DEVICE

Open this page on your phone and laptop.

After steps 1 and 2 succeed, press the button below. It saves only the public connection details on this device, then opens Governor. Your notes and actions stay in place.

In Governor, open Settings & backups (the gear on a phone), expand Use my existing email and password, and sign in. Wait for Cloud up to date.

Repeat only step 3 on your other device. Capture “Sync test” on one device and check that it appears on the other. Until both devices show Cloud up to date, they save separately.

Before a long absence, export a backup from Governor and keep it in two private places. Free cloud projects can pause when unused; your device copy still works.
