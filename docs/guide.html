A GUIDE THAT TAKES TWO MINUTES

Your life. A little clearer.

The Governor is a place to choose the next useful action, park the noise and remember what is progressing. You don’t have to fill it in every day.

Daily: Today / the Tunnel · 2–5 minutes

  1. Read the highlighted action. Edit the starter examples to match your actual BAUER and KAITECH work.
  2. Put extra tasks in Today → Parked priorities → Add parked priority. Keep three in focus. Finish that group, then Choose next priorities lets you select up to three more. Parked work has Supports and stays out of progress until completed.
  3. Choose at most three priorities. Keep each next action concrete: “check the data IDs” is enough.
  4. Press Complete action when it is done. Use Undo if you tapped accidentally.
  5. When your mind wanders, capture the thought and return. The focus timer and end-of-day check-in are optional.

On a difficult day, select Low energy. During a constrained week, select Maintenance week. Only one action stays in focus. Nothing else is deleted or penalized.

Weekly: Plans / the Compass · about 15 minutes

Review what actually moved, what made action difficult and what you will change next week. Update milestones using evidence, not how busy you felt. Your monthly record keeps completed and planned actions, check-ins and reviews. Set your military date only when it is useful; change it whenever necessary.

Write your own 1-, 3- and 5-year reasons. These are a direction, not a comparison with another person.

Whenever useful: Library / the Vault

Your reference shelf covers BAUER/APM, KAITECH/BIM, Excel, AI-free automation, English and public speaking, logic and sales conversations, fitness, sleep, attention, reading, history and economics. Use a card when it serves a real action. Save a note or link, then leave.

Earlier projects, habits, finances, fitness records and other categories remain accessible under Preserved Governor records after import. They do not become extra daily tracking obligations.

Your own areas

Open Settings → Manage areas, or press Edit beside MAIN AREAS on a laptop. Add a name and icon, rename an existing area, or remove its shortcut. Restore it whenever useful. Notes and goals stay saved when you rename or remove an area; your changes are included in backups and sync with your signed-in account.

Goals, calendar and books

In Plans, add or edit a goal’s outcome, next action, target date and monthly milestones. Use an entered percentage, a measurable target, or checked milestones. Change its status to pause, complete or archive it; use the status filter to find and reactivate an old goal. History keeps earlier versions. Deleting a goal asks for confirmation and retains its history and linked actions.

Tap a calendar day to schedule an action. Editing its date keeps the earlier planned record. The two charts count real completed actions and actual goal progress. In Library, notes are grouped by category. Add a book and optionally track its pages, or add a flexible routine and check its days in Plans. Monthly reviews and core values are optional.

Missed days

There is no overdue counter or broken streak. Use Choose from parked actions to bring back something that still matters. Earlier planned records stay in their original months.

Your phone and laptop

Open the same hosted app address on both devices. For an app icon, use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or Chrome’s Install app / Add to Home screen on Android. Bookmark it on your laptop.

Cloud sync needs your own free project and the same login on both devices. Until you connect it, each device keeps a separate local copy. Follow the connection guide.

Back up once a month

Open Settings & backups → Export backup. Save the JSON file somewhere private. To restore, choose Import backup; the app downloads a pre-import backup and merges records. The new app also accepts JSON exports from both earlier versions. It cannot read another website’s storage automatically; export from that website first.

Updates

If an older design still appears, open the safe update link while online. It refreshes app files and keeps your saved records and sign-in. Do this on each device. Do not clear browser data.

Offline

Visit online once before relying on offline reopening. After installation, local tasks, captures, reviews and notes work offline; edits sync later when connected. First sign-in, sync and outside links need internet. Browser storage may be cleared, so keep exports. Offline reopening was tested in Chromium; check your own phone before military service.

Use your own devices. Signing out opens a separate local workspace and hides your account in the app. Signing in again restores it. Browser copies are not encrypted. The site’s interface may be public, while the cloud records are restricted to your login.

Second Brain records and independent accounts

Export JSON from your Second Brain, then Governor Settings → Import backup. Tasks, goals, books, routines, notes, values and reviews become editable here; the complete original is preserved. Imported extra goals start paused.

Each person uses a separate login. Settings explains the optional one-time cloud permissions update for additional accounts. Export local-only records before sign-in, then import them into that account if needed. Never share your password or private backup.

Connect your day to your plans

Every saved plan is available in a priority’s Supports menu, including paused plans. Choose Other for work that does not support a plan. Create a plan in Plans and choose 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, 3 years or 5+ years; set its start date.

The Plans progress chart defaults to Days in horizon: days with at least one completed linked priority divided by the plan’s total days. Three completed priorities on the same day still count as one day for that plan. Other only affects daily execution. Completion dates within the plan’s start/end period count; future and undated imported completions do not. Calendar years include leap days; 5+ years uses a five-year window. The optional target date does not shorten the selected horizon. Blank days have no penalties.

Reopening, deleting or changing a priority’s Supports updates the count. Rescheduling preserves earlier completed work; incomplete records do not count. Change the chart to Outcome targets for your existing milestones, targets and estimates. Those measurements stay saved separately.

To remove a thought, open Your captured thoughts and tap Delete. You can also delete archived thoughts or delete from the thought editor. Undo restores it.
