ONE-TIME SETUP · NO PAID PLAN REQUIRED

One private copy, across devices.

The app is free and needs no AI subscription. Supabase stores a small private copy so your phone and laptop can share it. Creating the account requires you; do not send your password or secret keys to anyone.

Keep every account on its Free plan. Do not add a payment method or enable an upgrade. Free providers have limits and can change their terms. If a free cloud project pauses, your saved local copy still works.

1. Create your free cloud project

  1. Visit Supabase and create a Free project. Choose a strong database password and keep it in your password manager. It does not go into this app.
  2. In Authentication → Users, add your own email/password user. Use the same email as the account that owns the project if relying on Supabase’s built-in test emails.
  3. In Authentication → Providers / Email, disable new user signups after adding yourself. You are the only user.

2. Protect your records

Open the supplied setup.sql file. Replace all three copies of YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your login email. Paste the file into Supabase’s SQL Editor and click Run. This creates a new private table; it leaves the earlier candidate’s table untouched.

The row-level security rules allow only your email and your account ID. Never disable them. For testing, another account must not be able to read or write your row.

3. Connect the app

  1. Find your Project URL and publishable key in Supabase’s project connection / API settings. A publishable key starts with sb_publishable_; the older public anon key also works.
  2. Open the Governor → Settings & backups → Cloud connection. Paste these two public details and save. Never paste a secret or service-role key.
  3. Expand Use my existing email and password and sign in. Your password is sent to your project over HTTPS and is not saved by the app.
  4. Wait for Cloud up to date. On your other device, open the same app address, enter the same public connection details and sign in as yourself.

If using emailed sign-in links instead, set your deployed app URL under Authentication → URL configuration → Site URL and Redirect URLs. Open the link in the same browser/device that requested it. Built-in email delivery has restrictions and rate limits; password sign-in avoids depending on those emails.

4. Test your actual devices

  1. Capture “Sync test” on the laptop and wait for Cloud up to date.
  2. Open the phone app, press Sync now in Settings and confirm the thought appears in the Vault inbox.
  3. Make a second note on the phone, reconnect the laptop and confirm it arrives.
  4. Load the phone app online once, switch to airplane mode, reopen it and complete an action. Reconnect and confirm the change on the laptop.
  5. Export a backup. Keep your old app until its exported records are imported and checked.

Independent accounts — optional

Your existing owner account needs no repeated setup. For another person to save their own cloud copy, export your backup, then open Governor → Settings → Enable separate cloud accounts. Press Copy setup, open the storage setup, paste and Run. This runs multi-user.sql, preserving existing rows while permitting each authenticated account to read/write only its own row.

Create or invite the other account under Authentication → Users. Each person uses their own email/password; never share your login. Keep signups disabled if you only want invited accounts. Password sign-in avoids Supabase's built-in email recipient limits. Sign in on both of your own devices using the same account.

The app stores a separate local profile for each project/account. Signing out hides that account from the interface and opens a local workspace. Signing in restores its copy. To move local-only records into your account, export them first and explicitly import after sign-in. Browser copies and exported backups are not encrypted; use a locked personal device.

Verify with two real accounts: create a private note in account A, sign into B, and confirm it is absent. In a browser console using B's authenticated client, selecting A's user_id must return no row, and inserting/updating A's row must be denied. These live authorization checks have not been performed by the coding agent.

Your free app address

Use the existing Governor website on laptop and phone. It is hosted on GitHub Pages from governor-site at the root. No additional hosting account, domain or paid plan is needed.

Long breaks and backups

A free Supabase project can pause after inactivity. Check its dashboard if sync stops and restore/resume the project as its current policy permits. Never rely on a free cloud project as your only backup during military service. Export before leaving and monthly when practical. Store a second private copy somewhere you already use.

See current provider terms: Supabase pricing, Cloudflare Pages limits, GitHub Pages limits. Official provider documentation checked on October 8, 2026 lists a 500 MB Supabase database and 5 GB egress, and 500 Cloudflare Pages builds per month. The app uses a small document per account and a small static website. Free terms can change, and low activity over a seven-day period may pause Supabase; keep independent exports before long absences.
